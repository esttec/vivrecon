Frequently asked questions

Is Vivrecon free?

Yes — you can start budgeting for free. A premium plan adds extra features.

How much should I save each month?

A common target is 20% of your income. Vivrecon shows your exact number from the 50/30/20 rule.

Does it connect to my bank?

No bank login needed — you add expenses yourself, and that awareness is what saves money.

What is the 50/30/20 rule?

Split income 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. Use the calculator above to see yours.
